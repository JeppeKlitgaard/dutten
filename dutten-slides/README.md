# Dutten Slides

Unofficial slide template that somewhat matches the Design Guides of the Technical University of Denmark.

It lends its name from the common pet name given to Techincal University Of Denmark, DTU.

## Design Considerations

- Prefer to take space from right margin over left margin
  - This deviates from the Design Guideline, where the DTU logo is placed to the left
- Bottom edge has the least valuable space since this is more frequently blocked by students in front, tables, etc
  - Store information here
- Should be configurable to use official DTU colors in both CMYK (printing) and RGB (display) variations.
- Ability to track progress and breaks via bottom bar
- If both level 1 and level 2 headings are used, level 1 heading title carries information about 'chapter' of the presentation, similar to PowerPoint sections
